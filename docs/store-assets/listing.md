# Play Store listing text

## App name (30 max)
Rush Rivals

## Short description (80 max — this one is 74)
Dash over trains, dodge barriers & grab coins in a fast 3D city runner!

## Full description (4000 max)
Rush Rivals is a fast, colourful 3D endless runner. Swipe to switch lanes, jump over barriers, slide under gantries and run up ramps onto the train roofs, all while collecting as many coins as you can.

RUN, DODGE, COLLECT
• Three lanes, fast swipe controls and a camera that keeps the action readable.
• Ramps lead up onto train roofs, so take the high route to skip the traffic.
• Moving trains flash red, so watch out!

POWER-UPS
• Coin Magnet pulls nearby coins to you.
• Shield absorbs one crash.
• Speed Boost is a burst of unstoppable speed.
• Score x2 doubles your score.
• Hoverboards save you from one crash per run.

CHARACTERS, BOARDS & WORLDS
• Unlock six runners: Rush, Zoe, Kai, Shadow, Nova and Leo.
• Ride six boards, from Flame to Galaxy.
• Explore six worlds: City, Beach, Snow, Night City, Temple and Lava.

DAILY FUN
• Daily rewards and missions.
• Achievements to chase.
• Live events like Temple Rush.

PLAY OFFLINE, SYNC ONLINE
• Every run works offline.
• Create a free account (or sign in with Google) to back up your progress, sync it between devices and climb the daily, weekly and all-time leaderboards.

FAIR & FAMILY-FRIENDLY
• Free to play, with optional rewarded ads and in-app purchases.
• Under-13 child mode turns off personalised ads, needs a parent's check before any purchase, and keeps online features to a minimum.
